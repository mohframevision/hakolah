## About the Bakery

A regular neighborhood bakery in Abu Quwah — the go-to spot for plain
roti bread at 100 fils.

The bakery belongs to the Al Jazeera bakeries chain, founded in 1998, with branches in North Sehla,
Nuwaidrat, Sitra, Al Eker, Salmabad and Saar.

## Known For

**The stuffed pocket** — a quick, palm-sized snack, open on top with
different fillings inside (chicken, or labneh and zaatar, or a burger-style
filling), priced around 150 fils. A great option when you're suddenly
hungry and want something fast.

## Beyond Bread

- French pastries
- Arabic and Middle Eastern sweets, such as baklava
- Western sweets and cakes
- Orders for parties and occasions

For orders: 33900272, or via the
[bakery's Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/aljazeera.bakery/).
