## About the Bakery

Al Manar Bakeries is a well-known Bahraini bakery chain with multiple
branches across Bahrain — Diplomatic Area, Riffa Al Sharqi, Jid Ali, Jidhafs,
Al Budaiya, A'ali, Al Hajiyat, Al Khamis, and more.

## What It Offers

A variety of fresh, delicious baked goods.

## How to Order

Direct delivery through its own app, as well as through delivery platforms.
Also available on the App Store and Google Play with a dedicated ordering
app.

## Branches

Diplomatic Area, Riffa Al Sharqi, Jid Ali, Jidhafs, Al Budaiya, A'ali, Al
Hajiyat, Al Khamis, and other locations.
