## About the Bakery

Al Manar Bakeries is a well-known Bahraini bakery chain with branches across
Bahrain: Diplomatic Area, Riffa Al Sharqi, Jid Ali, Jidhafs,
Al Budaiya, A'ali, Al Hajiyat, Al Khamis and more.

## What It Offers

Fresh bread and baked goods.

## How to Order

Order for delivery through the bakery's own app (App Store and Google Play)
or through delivery platforms.

## Branches

Diplomatic Area, Riffa Al Sharqi, Jid Ali, Jidhafs, Al Budaiya, A'ali, Al
Hajiyat, Al Khamis, and other locations.
