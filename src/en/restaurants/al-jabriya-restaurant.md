## About the Restaurant

Al Jabriya is a Turkish-Bahraini restaurant with branches in Busaiteen,
Jidhafs, and other areas. It serves Turkish grills and shawarma with multiple
bread options (saj, Turkish, Lebanese), alongside pizza and pastries.

## Known For

**The Russian sandwich** — the dish most associated with the restaurant's
name, and a major reason for its wide popularity. Prices are mid-range and
accessible to most, though the restaurant sometimes gets crowded due to
demand.

## Branches

Busaiteen, Jidhafs, and other locations.
