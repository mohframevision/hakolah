## About the Restaurant

Al Shola is one of the best shawarma restaurants in Bahrain — a long-known
spot that holds a special place for many customers since childhood.

## Known For

**Arabic chicken shawarma** — the favorite dish here, with a taste that's
hard to put into words. Many customers agree the shawarma overall is
consistently good. It currently costs around 2.3 BHD (subject to change).
