## About the Restaurant

Adam Subs is a burger and subs restaurant, with delivery via several apps.

## Known For

**The Crisper burger (beef)** — the favorite dish here: caramelized fried
onions over a freshly cooked patty (not a pre-made solid one), and the mix
of caramelized onion with the beef gives a distinct taste that's hard to
describe. The **Zinger** is a strong option too. One of the best places
for burgers and fillet steak.
