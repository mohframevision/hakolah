## About the Restaurant

Joody's is a new restaurant that opened in Sehla, serving sandwiches and
fast food under the slogan "Eat good, feel jood" — prices that work for
one person or a whole group.

## Known For

**Cheap, tasty sandwiches.** I was a customer here before I filmed an ad
for them, and I went inside the kitchen myself during filming — that
confirmed the restaurant's cleanliness and real quality, not just what
they say. They're constantly improving, and they can supply large
quantities for a gathering or event order.
