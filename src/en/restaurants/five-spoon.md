## About the Restaurant

Five Spoon serves Italian, Chinese, and American food in Jid Ali — an
upscale atmosphere that suits families and younger crowds alike.

## Known For

The **pasta** dishes especially, with a good variety across Italian,
Chinese, and American plates. Prices are very reasonable for the quality,
the owner personally looks after customers, and the staff are friendly. A
place worth treating yourself at with good, well-balanced food.
