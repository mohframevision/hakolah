## What Is Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow is an AI-powered voice dictation app that works on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. The core idea is simple: you speak, and the app turns your speech into clean, organized written text, instead of typing it by hand on a keyboard.

## What Sets It Apart from Traditional Voice Typing

The app doesn't just transcribe speech word-for-word like ordinary speech-to-text tools — it understands the intent behind a sentence, automatically removes filler words (like "um" and "uh") and unintended repetition, and adds proper punctuation and formatting (paragraphs, lists, emails). It also learns the user's personal vocabulary over time, such as names and recurring terms, and supports over a hundred languages with automatic detection as you switch between them mid-speech. It works inside any app you can type in, without needing plugins or per-app setup.

## Plans and Pricing

The free plan's limit varies by device: 2,000 words per week on desktop (Mac and Windows), and 1,000 words per week on iPhone. On Android, dictation is unlimited as of this writing (August 2026), though the company itself described this as a "limited time" offer tied to the Android app's launch, so it may later be capped like the other platforms. The paid Pro plan offers unlimited dictation on every device permanently, and there are also custom plans for teams and organizations.
