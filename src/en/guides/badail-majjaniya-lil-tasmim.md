## No Adobe Subscriptions? That Doesn't Stop You From Starting

Professional design and editing software, like Photoshop, After Effects, Cinema 4D, and Premiere Pro, are undoubtedly powerful tools, but most of them rely on monthly or yearly subscriptions that can be a real burden for beginners and independent content creators. The good news is there are genuine alternatives to them, and some have recently become completely free.

## Photoshop Alternative: Affinity

**Affinity**, acquired by Canva, used to be sold as a one-time purchase with no subscription. In late 2025, Canva merged its three apps (Photo, Designer, and Publisher) into a single app called **Affinity Studio**, and made it **completely free**. Photo editing, vector design, and page layout are all 100% free, and the only thing that requires a paid subscription is the AI tools, which need a Canva Pro subscription.

## After Effects Alternative: Cavalry

**Cavalry** is a 2D motion graphics app built on a node-based system, and after Canva's acquisition it became **completely free for individuals**, even for commercial use (except for companies that need team features and SSO login, which still requires payment). It's not a "lite" version of After Effects — it was built from scratch for high-performance procedural animation.

## Cinema 4D Alternative: Blender

**Blender** has been a completely free and open-source 3D app for many years, not the result of a recent acquisition. It offers modeling, animation, rendering, and even basic editing, all within one completely free tool.

## Premiere Pro Alternative: DaVinci Resolve

**DaVinci Resolve** by Blackmagic Design offers a genuinely free version, with no watermark or time limit, covering professional editing, color correction, Fusion effects, and audio editing, at quality up to 4K at 60 frames per second. The paid version, **DaVinci Resolve Studio**, costs $295 as a **one-time purchase only**, with no subscription, and adds AI tools, higher resolution up to 32K, higher frame rates up to 120fps, and cloud collaboration.

## Conclusion

If your budget is limited, you can start today with completely free tools that cover most design, motion graphics, editing, and 3D needs, and move to paid versions later only when you actually need an advanced feature not available in the free version.
