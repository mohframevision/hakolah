## When You're in a Hurry

Most people looking for a file app are in a tight spot: a file they need right now and can't find, a video too big to send, or an archive they don't know how to open. So this guide is organized by problem, not by app name. Every app here is free, and most are open source.

## Looking for a Lost File? Everything

Windows' built-in search is slow and often returns unrelated results. **Everything** indexes every file on your PC in seconds, then shows results instantly as you type, even on drives with millions of files. Its only limits: it searches file names, not their contents, and it's Windows-only.

## Need to Shrink a Video? HandBrake or Shutter Encoder

To shrink a large video while keeping its quality, use **HandBrake**: pick your file, choose a preset, and press start. If you need pro formats like ProRes or finer control, **Shutter Encoder** offers that with an easy interface. Both are built on **FFmpeg**, the engine running behind most video apps, which you can also use directly from the command line.

## An Archive File? 7-Zip

**7-Zip** opens ZIP, RAR, ISO and more, compresses folders with a password, and is genuinely free with no purchase reminders. If you use WinRAR, make sure it's updated to version 7.13 or newer, since it doesn't update itself.

## A Video That Won't Play? VLC

If your default media player refuses a file, **VLC** will most likely play it: it supports almost every format, plus subtitle sync and file conversion.

## Moving Files Between Phone and PC

Instead of emailing files to yourself, **LocalSend** works like AirDrop between any two devices on the same Wi-Fi, whether Windows, Mac, Android or iPhone. To keep whole folders in sync across your devices without a middleman company, use **Syncthing**.

## Uninstalling Programs Completely

When you uninstall a program on Windows, files and registry entries usually stay behind. **Revo Uninstaller** runs the program's own uninstaller, then scans for leftovers and removes them.

## Bottom Line

Every file problem has a small free app that solves it: Everything to search, HandBrake to shrink, 7-Zip to compress, VLC to play, LocalSend to transfer. The full list with links is on the [Files & PC Utilities](/en/links-tools/files.html) page.
