## Why Do AI Video Creators Need a Tool Like This?

When generating video with AI tools like Kling, Runway, or Seedance, precisely specifying the camera movement (tracking, panning, zoom, dolly...) inside the text prompt makes a big difference in the result, but many users don't know the correct cinematography terms, or how to describe a specific movement in words the tool understands.

## What Exactly Does the Tool Offer

AI Camera Movements provides a reference library of 46 video clips showing different camera movements, organized into 7 categories: Pan/Tilt, Zoom/Lens, Dolly/Track, Drone/Crane, Physical Moves, Human Camera, and Specials. Each clip comes with a ready-made prompt that separates the camera movement instructions from the scene details, so users can swap out just the first frame while keeping the movement instructions intact. The tool also lets you preview looping clips to compare movements by direction, speed, and framing, and copy the prompt directly for use in video generation platforms like Kling, Seedance, Higgsfield, and Runway.

## Is the Tool Free?

Yes, it's completely free, and aimed specifically at AI-driven filmmakers and video creators.
