## Start With a Question: What Do You Want to Do With Audio?

"Audio app" covers very different jobs: cleaning up a voice recording, composing electronic music, recording a full band, routing game and chat audio while streaming. Each one has a free app that fits it better than the rest.

## To Record and Clean Up Your Voice: Audacity

**Audacity** has been the go-to for podcasts and voiceovers for over twenty-five years. Record, cut, remove background noise, normalize volume, then export to MP3 or WAV. It's an audio editor, not a music studio, so don't expect virtual instruments.

## To Make Music and Beats: LMMS

If you want to try making music before paying for FL Studio, **LMMS** is a great place to start: a piano roll for melodies, a beat editor, built-in sounds, and VST plugin support. Its limit is that it's built around MIDI and patterns, so it isn't made for recording vocals or real instruments.

## To Record Instruments and Vocals Professionally: Ardour

Once you're past the hobby stage, **Ardour** offers a professional recording and mixing studio: unlimited tracks, non-destructive editing and full automation. The code is open source, and the developers ask for a small optional payment for the ready-made installer to fund development.

## To Route Audio While Streaming: Voicemeeter

Windows gives you one input and one output. **Voicemeeter** turns your PC into a virtual mixing desk: game audio into the recording, voice chat out of it, music only in your headphones.

## To Name That Song: Shazam

**Shazam** identifies any song playing around you with one tap, even in a noisy place, and it's free with no ads.

## What About Sound Design?

Sound design is creating effects and soundscapes for films, games and videos. You can start by generating sounds with LMMS's synths, then process and layer them in Audacity. Hakolah's [Sounds page](/en/sounds.html) also offers audio tools that run right in your browser with nothing to install.

## Bottom Line

Audacity for podcasts, LMMS to start making music, Ardour for pro recording, Voicemeeter for streaming. The full list with links is on the [Audio & Music](/en/links-tools/audio.html) page.
