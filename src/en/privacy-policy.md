# Privacy Policy

## Data we currently collect

The site, in its current version, doesn't require login and doesn't collect personal data that identifies you by name. The "Favorites" feature is saved only inside your browser (using localStorage) and is never sent to any server or third party.

We use Google Analytics to see general visit statistics (number of visitors, most-visited pages) to improve the site — this data is aggregated and not tied to your personal identity.

## Notifications (optional)

If you enable the "Notify me daily" button on the homepage, we only store the technical subscription data (a device-specific link generated by your browser) on our own server that we manage ourselves — **with no third party** seeing this data or sitting in the notification's path. We use it only to send one daily notification with "today's pick." You can turn it off anytime with a single button click from the same place, and we delete your subscription data immediately.

## Microphone (optional)

The Hakolah Sounds page asks for microphone access only when you press a record button yourself (to record your voice or an instrument you play, or to make an instrument from your voice). The recording stays on your device: it is saved in your browser only, never uploaded to any server, and never included in project share links. You can delete it at any time from the page itself, or revoke microphone access in your browser settings.

## Abuse prevention

To stop automated scripts from faking the like counter, our own server stores a
**hashed fingerprint of the network address** when you press the like button only
— we never store the IP address itself, the hash cannot be reversed, and it is
deleted automatically within two minutes. It is used for one purpose only:
checking whether the same device has hit a per-minute limit on likes.

## Cookies and ads

We save your preferences (such as theme and favorites) in your browser with cookies, which the site needs to work. Visit measurement through Google Analytics and personalization of Google ads based on your visits to this site and others are only turned on after you consent in the banner shown on your first visit; if you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and ads stay non-personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, and manage Google ads through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## External links

The site contains links to external sites (tools, restaurants, stores, social media accounts). We're not responsible for the privacy policies or content on those sites. Some of these links (such as the Casa Souq link) are affiliate links — we may earn a small commission if you purchase through them, at no extra cost to you, and this is also noted next to the link itself.

## Your data rights

Under Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you have the right to access any personal data we hold about you, and to request its correction or deletion. You can exercise this right by contacting us as described below.

## Contact us

For any inquiry about this policy, you can reach us via the [contact page]({{ '/en/contact.html' | url }}) or directly by email: [mohframevision@outlook.com](mailto:mohframevision@outlook.com)
