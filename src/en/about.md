# About Hakolah

Hakolah is a Bahraini site for creators, in Arabic and English. It has free tools that run in your browser, experiments, and practical articles.

## What will you find on Hakolah?

- **[Hakolah Sounds](/en/sounds.html):** play, compose and learn music in your browser, with real recorded instruments.
- **[Dilmo](/en/dilmo.html):** a small companion you play with and talk to.
- **[Experiments and tools](/en/ai-experiments.html):** like the file converter, all running on your device.
- **[Free software and tools](/en/links-tools.html):** hand-picked links for editing, design, audio and coding.
- **[Articles and guides](/en/guides.html):** carefully written guides, such as protecting your accounts.

<h2 id="torilu">Where did the places guide go?</h2>

The guide to restaurants, cafes, stores and places, with menus, search, "Pick for me" and "Near me", moved in November 2026 to its own site, [Torilu](https://torilu.com/en/index.html). Old links take you to the same page there, and your favorites move with you.

## Is the current content final?

No. The site keeps growing with new tools and improvements to existing ones.

<h2 id="ads">Ads and paid links</h2>

We don't show ads. Some links are **referral links** that earn us a small commission if you buy through them, such as the Casa Souq link, and we say so clearly next to them.

<h2 id="privacy">Your privacy</h2>

Hakolah has no accounts or passwords, and we don't collect your personal data. Full details are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy.html). If you'd like to learn how to protect your own accounts, we have a [detailed guide](/en/guides/himaya-hisabatik-alraqmiya.html).

<h2 id="credits">Sound credits</h2>

Hakolah Sounds uses recorded instrument samples under open licenses. Full details are in the [credits file](/assets/audio/CREDITS.txt).
