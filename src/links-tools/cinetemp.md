title_en: CineTemp
desc_en: |-
  An iPhone app that turns your phone's camera into a professional light meter for filmmakers — measures color temperature (Kelvin) for any light source directly through the camera, and calculates exposure and ND filter values. It also includes an AR sun tracker showing the sun's path and golden/blue hour times for any location and date, very useful during location scouting and pre-production.

  Works fully offline with no account or data collection, designed by cinematographer Keaton Nye specifically for filmmakers. Note: paid app ($19.99), not free.
