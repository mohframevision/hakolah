title_en: Sahehly (Arabic Proofreading)
desc_en: |-
  An Arabic-language platform specialized in AI-powered proofreading and automatic diacritics (tashkeel), by Sakhr Software. Corrects spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors with explanations, plus multiple diacritics modes (full, partial, mandatory, word-ending only). Also offers a Quran word-search assistant, number-to-Arabic-text conversion, and Arabic-English translation on paid plans. Used by students, writers, editors, and institutions, with partners including King Abdulaziz University, the Saudi Ministry of Education, and STC.

  Plans: Free (up to 1,500 characters/day), Professional ($8.99/month, up to 100,000 words, includes translation and OCR), Business (custom institutional pricing).
